Incremental-validity claims for test-adequacy metrics are not resolvable
A behavioural feature block's R2 gain has a 95% interval width of 0.06-0.15
Project-level confounding alone manufactures false increments in up to 82% of runs
Fixed effects hold that rate below 1% on two benchmarks in two languages
Resolving a 0.05 increment needs ~70-100 fault versions, 0.02 needs ~100-150
